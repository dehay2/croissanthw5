Croissant🥐

🥐Fun Fact

Croissants are a symbol of French baking expertise and are celebrated worldwide. In France, there are even annual competitions to crown the best croissant in Paris. Croissants were once considered a luxury item for the wealthy but are now a common breakfast staple. They come in sweet and savory varieties, including chocolate, almond, ham and cheese, or spinach and feta.
